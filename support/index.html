Support

Questions, a bug, or a deal that feels unfair? Write to support@quiethandofficial.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads on another iPhone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is every deal winnable? With "Winnable deals only" on (the default), every new deal has a winning line the game has already found. Turn it off in Settings for fully random deals.

Where are my games and statistics stored? On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

When do ads appear? In the free version, an ad may appear between games, never during a hand.
