Quiet Hand

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Quiet Hand is a Spider Solitaire game for iPhone published by Criccletech Bilisim Limited Sirketi ("we").

What stays on your iPhone

Your games, statistics, daily streak and settings are stored on your device only. Quiet Hand has no account and does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

Ads

The free version shows ads between games, never during a hand. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from your IP address), ad interactions and diagnostic information. Quiet Hand does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps, so your advertising ID is not used to personalise ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads and lets you change that choice later in Settings. See Google's explanation of how it uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Removing ads

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase stops every ad request. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Children

Quiet Hand is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the date above will be updated.
